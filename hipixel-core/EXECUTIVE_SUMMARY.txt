TENSOR PIPELINE
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

VideoFrame (H,W,3) float32 [0,1]
    ↓
Transpose HWC → NCHW + batch
    ↓ (1, 3, H, W)
backend.run({"input": tensor}, key)
    ↓
Output dict: {"output": (1, 3, H*scale, W*scale)}
    ↓
Transpose NCHW → HWC
    ↓ (H*scale, W*scale, 3)
Clip [0,1]
    ↓
NEW VideoFrame with updated dimensions


TILE INFERENCE PATTERN (RealESRGAN reference)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

For each tile (512px × 512px with 32px overlap):
  1. Extract tile with padding (clamped to bounds)
  2. Transpose to NCHW, run backend.run()
  3. Accumulate output with weight matrix
  ↓
Normalize overlaps (divide by weight)
  ↓
NO SEAMS! Weighted averaging handles boundary perfectly
